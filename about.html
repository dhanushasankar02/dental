Our Story • Since 2008

A clinic built on listening,
not just looking.

SmileCare started in a two-chair room above a bakery in 2008. No investors, no marketing budget — just a dentist who believed that if you explain things honestly, patients come back. Eighteen years later, that belief still runs everything we do.

Milestones

Eighteen years, one conviction.

Every step was earned by patients who trusted us and told their friends.

2008

The first chair

Dr. Anil Menon opens SmileCare above a bakery with one assistant and a second-hand X-ray machine. Six patients on day one.

2013

Digital first

We became one of the first clinics in the state to go fully digital — intraoral scanners, digital X-rays, paperless records.

2017

Second clinic, bigger team

Opened the second clinic and added dedicated orthodontics, endodontics, and paediatric departments.

2021

In-house dental lab

Brought crown and aligner fabrication in-house — same-day crowns became possible and pricing dropped 30%.

2026

Three clinics, same promise

Over 18,000 treatments completed. The bakery downstairs is gone — we bought the whole building.

What We Stand For

Four ideas that shape every decision.

Comfort First

If a treatment is uncomfortable, it's on us to fix — not on you to endure.

Plain Language

No jargon, no fear-selling. You'll always know what we're doing and why.

Evidence-Based

Every recommendation traces back to peer-reviewed research — not habit.

Do Less, Better

We'd rather preserve your natural tooth than sell you an implant. Always.

We treat teeth. We care for people.

Dentistry has a reputation problem — cold chairs, rushed appointments, bills that appear out of nowhere. We built SmileCare as the opposite of that: warm rooms, unhurried consults, and pricing you see before you sit down.

Our team spends an average of 40 minutes with every new patient — most of it listening. Because a treatment plan that ignores your fears, your schedule, or your budget isn't really a plan at all.

A team that actually likes each other.

We hire slowly and fire rarely. Every dentist, assistant, and front-desk team member at SmileCare has been here at least three years — because continuity is the quiet superpower of good dental care.

Friday afternoons are for case reviews. Everyone's invited to question a diagnosis, suggest a different approach, or share a paper they read. It keeps us humble and it keeps our patients safer.
