Does a root canal hurt?
Modern rotary endodontics with profound local anesthesia means most patients report little to no discomfort — many return to work the same day.
Are clear aligners as effective as braces?
For most mild to moderate cases, yes. Severe rotations may still need fixed braces — our orthodontist will tell you honestly at the consult.
Do you accept insurance?
We work with all major dental insurers and offer zero-cost EMI on treatments above ₹15,000. Bring your policy card to your visit.
How often should I get a cleaning?
Every six months for most adults. If you smoke, have gum disease, or wear braces, we may recommend every three to four months.
Can I bring my child?
Absolutely — we have a dedicated paediatric suite and a dentist who specializes in making kids feel at ease. First checkup recommended by age one.
